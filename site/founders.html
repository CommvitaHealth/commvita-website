Why we started

We have been on both sides of this

commvita did not start with a technology. It started with a frustration anyone who has both sold into public health systems and worked inside them will recognise. The integration never quite arrives. The data is never quite current. And the contract is always harder to leave than it was to sign.

Founder & Chief Strategy Officer

Martin Carpenter

in

Sold digital health into public systems, then worked inside them. Frustrated by the integration that never arrived, by an industry competing on how hard it is to leave rather than on total cost, and by a sprawl of separate products no other real-time industry would put up with.

“No airline, bank or logistics operator would run on data that is weeks old. Health should not have to either.”

Co-founder & Chief Commercial Officer

Hassan Chaudhury

in

Sees digital health companies making the same promises to the same systems the world over, and the same systems buying the same disappointment. Works on the commercial side to make a different answer possible: priced so that adopting it is never the expensive decision, and built so that leaving is a decision you can actually make.

Co-founder & Chief Data & Analytics Officer

Professor Marc Farr

in

Here for three reasons: openEHR designed in from the start rather than bolted on; flow data brought together in a way health has not seen before; and health inequalities made something you can act on, not just report.

“Flow data and an open record in the same place changes what analytics can answer.”

commvita™

A health and care platform you set up to suit you. Built to open standards, configured for each country, and designed so you can leave.

What is not included

The half of a total cost of ownership that suppliers leave out

None of this is billed by commvita, and all of it is real money. A business case that omits it is beaten a year later by the costs nobody put in it.

Infrastructure and hosting

  • Compute, storage, network and backup. commvita is self-hosted by design — on your infrastructure or in your own cloud tenancy — so that bill goes to you and not through us.
  • Disaster recovery and a second site, where your resilience policy requires one.
  • Connectivity to national networks, and any charge that carries.
  • A commvita-hosted option exists and is priced separately. It is not in the figures on this page.

Third-party licences commvita cannot grant

  • SNOMED CT, licensed through a national affiliate arrangement in your own organisation’s name — free at the point of use in Member and low-income countries, which is exactly why commvita treats the edition as a per-jurisdiction binding rather than shipping one and assuming it fits.
  • ICD-11, LOINC and the wider ATC set, each pending a licence from its publisher. The adapters and the per-jurisdiction binding are built; what is outstanding is the licence and its credentials.
  • Any commercial terminology server, if you choose one over the national service.

Implementation and change

  • Data migration from the incumbent systems, which is scoped against what those systems will actually export rather than against what they hold.
  • Configuration and jurisdiction profile sign-off. The profile is a governed record, and its mandatory domains need a NAMED person to accept accountability. That person is yours, not ours.
  • Training delivery and, more materially, the backfill while people attend it. The platform ships the learning record; it does not ship the cover.
  • Business change and process redesign, which is where a consolidation either lands or does not.

Assurance you must hold yourself

  • Clinical safety officer time under DCB0129 and DCB0160. A hazard stays open until a named clinical safety officer records a decision, and that is an accountable human act rather than a workflow step.
  • Your DPIA, your information governance sign-off and your Caldicott review.
  • Organisation-level certifications commvita does not hold and does not claim — DAPB4121 at organisation level, TEFCA participation, ONC certification. Each is obtained by the deploying organisation.

Support, and the things people forget

  • Support is optional and separately priced on Flow. The £1 buys the licence, not a service desk.
  • Your own internal team. A platform still needs administrators, and this model reduces effort rather than removing it.
  • Third-party charges for access to systems you already pay for.

What we could not source

Stated rather than filled in

Three gaps, and why each is a gap rather than an oversight

The band tables. The pricing card publishes ranges — £28–55 per employee and £1.00–3.50 per head — and the workforce and population bands that place an organisation inside them are not published in this repository. This page therefore shows the range and does not pick a point inside it, because a single confident number would be the more useful thing to write and the less true one.

Every buyer-specific cost. Cost per whole-time equivalent, current licence spend, hosting and current establishment are all inputs, because this build environment cannot reach a source for any of them. Your ledger and your establishment are better sources than a national average in any case.

Realised savings. There are none to cite. The 80% figure on the rest of this site is an ambition for total cost of ownership and is worded that way everywhere it appears, including here. commvita launches today; nobody has banked it, and this page will keep saying so until somebody has.

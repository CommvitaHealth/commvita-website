Quality impact assessment

A gate, not a form. A scheme that has not been assessed for its effect on quality, safety, access and equity cannot be approved.

The gate

Assessing what a saving costs

Nine domains

Quality, safety, access, experience, equity, workforce, sustainability, statutory duty and clinical effectiveness.

Triggered, not optional

An assessment starts automatically for any scheme above a certain size, or one that affects a protected characteristic or a legal duty.

Refusals that mean something

You cannot submit while a required area is unassessed, and the refusal names exactly what is missing rather than just failing.

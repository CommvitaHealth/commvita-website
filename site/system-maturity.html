A diagnostic for where an organisation actually is, service line by service line, and what the next pound moves.

The ladder

Six rungs, and most organisations sit across two

Maturity models are useful as a diagnostic and misleading as a score. An organisation is rarely at one level; it’s at different levels by service line, and the useful question is which rung the next pound moves.

The ladder is a diagnostic, not a score.

HIMSS models

Maturity worked out from what the platform actually does, across eight HIMSS models. commvita measures how ready you’re and gathers the evidence. The formal stage is awarded by HIMSS — we never certify ourselves.

Left shift

Two of the instruments

Screenshots of the platform as it runs, build B-569. Both modules carry their own data-provenance banner, and it’s left in.

One index across the three shifts, with a pillar score for each and an equity modifier that pulls the headline down when the gains aren’t reaching everyone. Here it takes three points off because digital exclusion is rising in the over-75s and the most deprived quintile. A score that goes up while the gap widens isn’t progress, and the module says so on the front page.

The board scorecard asks the questions a board should ask: are people healthier, are they getting timely access, is the money going where it does most good, can staff work without friction. Every tile is a named query that reconciles with its own drill-through, so a number on the scorecard can be traced to the cases behind it. The pyramid underneath shows who needs support now and how many in each tier are getting worse. LeftShift Intelligence© sits inside this module as the Shift Maturity tab. Nothing is duplicated.

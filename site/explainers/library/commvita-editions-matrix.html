What is in which edition

Every module in the three commvita editions, in one grid. The editions are additive: the Commvita Flow Edition is the open base, Governance & Assurance layers the governance spine on top, and the Population Platform is the whole-system edition that includes governance & assurance in full. A tick therefore cascades to the right.

Live vs demonstrated: Live — real, API-backed platform logic (wired end-to-end today) Demonstrated — representative control surface with seeded data / illustrative UI mock-up

Every module listed below resolves to a real, routed module in the platform. Nothing on this page is a roadmap item. Where a module is a demonstrated control surface rather than live API-backed logic, that is stated inside the module itself, not hidden here.

Each figure counts named capabilities, cumulative because the editions are additive. Several capabilities can sit on one route — Flow Edition 139 capabilities across 139 routes; Governance & Assurance Edition 255 across 241; Population Platform 417 across 403 — so the two numbers are stated separately rather than conflated.

Flow is £1 per instance with optional paid support. It is not free, and it is not a trial. It is the open, self-hosted platform base on open standards (openEHR / FHIR / OMOP) with FDP-parity operational products — priced so the entry cost is never the objection.

Why Information Governance is not in Flow

It is tempting to argue that every deployment needs some IG surface, so the IG Hub — DSPT toolkit submission, DPIA register, the ICO 72-hour breach clock, the Caldicott eight principles and DSAR SLA tracking — belongs in the base edition. It does not.

Flow provides the platform primitives that IG is built on: identity and access management, the audit framework, data products and cataloguing. The IG Hub is a different thing — it is the accountability record an organisation is inspected against. Putting it in the base edition gives away the single most valuable artefact of the Governance & Assurance Edition, which is the commercial beachhead precisely because boards buy inspection readiness. IG Hub is therefore in Governance & Assurance and the Population Platform, and not in the Flow.

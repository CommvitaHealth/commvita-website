The VUIT partner layer — now live

VUIT is built and API-backed behind a fail-safe feature flag, and switched on across the UK nations + Ireland in this demo. It brings the voluntary & community sector onto the board four ways — a destination for support, an outcome contributor, an inequality reducer and a value contributor — through one normalised PartnerActivity adapter, so any partner source maps with no schema change.

Outcome Intelligence → Workforce Experience: the ● Live VUIT partner sub-section (referrals, acceptance, completion, partner-attributed outcomes, escalation avoided, social value) with the activity list it reconciles with.

Fail-safe by design

Off unless enabled

The flag defaults OFF; enabling is per-jurisdiction or global, admin-controlled. In this demo it’s on for the five nations above.

Research, end to end on OMOP

A cohort built in Clinical Studies now exports as an OMOP CDM v5.4 extract manifest and an OHDSI ATLAS (Circe) cohort definition — concept sets mapped from the cohort’s condition/drug/measurement filters (SNOMED→OMOP / dm+d→RxNorm / LOINC), aggregate-only and k≥5-suppressed, with an “Open in OHDSI ATLAS” hand-off. The Research Portal lets external researchers request the extract as OMOP CDM v5.4, FHIR R4 Bulk or CSV. Federated Cohort Discovery already queries OMOP-CDM nodes, so discovery → cohort → research all speak OMOP.

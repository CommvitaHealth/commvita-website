Care happens in real time. So why does the data about it turn up weeks later?

Every health system needs three things: a shared care record, a platform to run operations on, and somewhere the board can see what’s really going on. Most buy them separately. Procurement after procurement, integration after integration, and a team of people whose job is keeping the whole lot in step.

commvita is all three at once.

That reads like a slogan until you watch what it changes. An incident reported on a ward becomes a risk on the board’s register without anyone re-keying it. A delayed discharge becomes a flow action. A care gap becomes a recall. Same person, same data, three lenses.

It’s openEHR-aligned, with FHIR on the wire and SNOMED for the clinical terms. Your data stays in an open standard and you can export all of it. We’d like you to stay because the thing works, not because you can’t get out.

Over the next few months I’ll walk through it a function at a time — how flow gets managed, how assurance gets evidenced, how the record holds a whole person. A new post every three days.

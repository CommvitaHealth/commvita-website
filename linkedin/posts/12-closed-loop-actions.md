Every operations centre I’ve been in has a screen on the wall that nobody looks at by February. It showed the problem beautifully and you couldn’t do anything from it.

The difference between a dashboard and an operations platform is whether you can act from where you’re standing.

Closed-loop actions sit on the flow canvas. Find the constraint, then do something about it on the same screen: raise the discharge task, notify the GP through GP Connect or the national record locator, book the transport, order the medicines to take away. Those actions reach across acute, community and social care, because a discharge-to-assess constraint is never solved inside the hospital reporting it.

The Discharge Hub is where that work gets held — planning, the OPTICA view, who’s waiting on what, and the delay coded by reason, so the weekly meeting can be about causes instead of about the total.

Constraint detection on the canvas runs live. The action surface is a demonstration today, and I’d sooner tell you that than have you find out in a demo.

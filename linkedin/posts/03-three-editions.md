People ask which bit of commvita they should buy. The honest answer is: whichever part of the problem is hurting.

There are three editions. They’re additive, and each one is complete on its own.

**commvita Flow** is the operational base. You can’t see the whole system, the front door is under pressure, and nobody agrees where the constraint is. Flow matches what the NHS Federated Data Platform does and puts an operational layer on top — self-hosted, on open standards. £1 per instance, one deployment for your organisation, every environment included. It isn’t free; it’s priced so that adopting the platform is never the expensive decision.

**Governance & Assurance** is the edition boards buy. An inspection is coming, the board can’t evidence its own controls, and the risk register doesn’t reconcile with what happened. It carries no clinical workflow at all, so it needs no clinical accreditation to land.

**Population** is where you end up. The clinical record across every setting — primary care, community, mental health, social care, pharmacy, dentistry — with the population layer reading it. Longest adoption curve of the three, which is why it usually follows one of the other two.

Adding the next edition re-implements nothing. It’s the same platform underneath.

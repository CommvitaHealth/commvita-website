Every winter there’s a system call where eight organisations each report their own pressure and nobody can say which one is holding the line back.

The Health Flow Canvas draws the whole health economy as a single production line. Prevention dam, front gate, ED, AMU, base ward, therapy, discharge to assess, exit — with a returns loop carrying failure demand, the bounce-backs and readmissions, back to intake, so rework is visible instead of buried in the totals.

One law binds it. Work in progress equals arrival rate times time in the system: Little’s Law, per station, on the canvas. Queue, WIP, λ and W, with a whole-system pressure index alongside them.

Then it does the thing a dashboard won’t. It finds the binding constraint — the one station capping everybody — and animates the back-pressure starving whatever sits downstream. Switch the scope to Medicine, Surgery, Frailty, Stroke or Elective and the line re-flows, because the constraint moves when you change what you’re looking at.

Where this sits today: flow, WIP, queue and constraint run on a live model built from real ward, delay and OPEL state. Demand and returns stay representative until those feeds connect, and the screen tells you so.

Where to look: /flow.

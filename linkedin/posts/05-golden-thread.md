A ward sister reports an incident on a Tuesday. Eleven weeks later a line appears on the board assurance framework. Ask anyone in the room to show you the events underneath that line, and watch what happens.

That gap is what the golden thread closes.

The chain runs Event → Theme → Systemic Issue → Risk → Objective. Each step is a promotion, and each promotion carries its evidence forward. The risk still points back at the systemic issue, the theme, and the individual incidents that made it. So a risk score always reconciles with the exact set of events beneath it, and “show me” takes a click.

Three things fall out of building it that way.

Operational, corporate and board risk stop being three registers. They’re three lenses over one source of truth, so they can’t disagree with each other.

Themes get found where incidents are reported. Patterns across the free-text safety record surface inside the workflow, with no separate analytics tool to license.

And de-escalation has to be earned. Closing a risk needs assurance plus an abating signal, never the calendar.

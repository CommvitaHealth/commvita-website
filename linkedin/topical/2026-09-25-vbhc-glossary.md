Commvita is adopting the VBHC Key Terms Glossary as its working vocabulary.

Not “conformance” — there’s nothing to certify against. It’s a glossary, and what you do with a glossary is use it, over and over, until the dashboard and the board paper mean the same thing by the same word.

That’s the problem it solves. Two people say “value” in a meeting and mean allocative value and cost-utility. Somebody says “variation” and nobody asks whether it’s warranted. A shared glossary makes those arguments harder to have by accident.

Credit where it belongs: it’s the work of Sir Muir Gray and Jane Johnston, prepared for NHSE and Frimley ICB by the Outcomes, Value and Stewardship Network with Data Observatory CIC, and the causal determinants appendix is by Anant Jani.

Where we already line up:

**Left shift.** The appendix asks whether a population is shifting left or right and plots the trajectory. We built LeftShift Intelligence to score that across digital, community and prevention, with an equity modifier that pulls the number down when the gains are unequal.

**Equity and equality.** Every access and outcome measure splits by Core20PLUS5 with gap indicators, and small numbers suppressed at five.

**Population healthcare and allocative value**, across segmentation and deprivation.

Where we don’t line up, which is the more useful half:

We have no programme budgeting and marginal analysis. We don’t separate warranted from unwarranted variation. We have no overuse measure, no waste measure, no cost-utility and no shared decision-making surface. And we use “stewardship” only in the antimicrobial sense, which isn’t the glossary’s sense at all.

Six gaps, named. They go on the roadmap in the glossary’s words instead of ours — which is rather the point of taking on somebody else’s vocabulary. You inherit their questions along with their nouns.

Marc Farr, our co-founder and chief data and analytics officer, is leading this for us. He’s also President of the Data Observatory, and our commercial co-founder Hassan Chaudhury chairs its National Council — so read the warm half of this with that in mind. The gap list is the part I’d trust.

We’ll publish where we stand term by term.

I read a glossary and found six gaps in our own product.

commvita is adopting the VBHC Key Terms Glossary — Sir Muir Gray and Jane Johnston’s work for NHS England and Frimley ICB — as our working vocabulary. Most of it we already speak. Some of it we don’t, and the document was quicker at finding that out than we were.

The one that stings: the glossary defines left shift as moving demand upstream while also cutting overuse, waste and futile care. We measure the first. We have no measure at all for the second. By its definition we’re doing half of the thing we named a product after.

The full piece has the other five, and the interest I have to declare.

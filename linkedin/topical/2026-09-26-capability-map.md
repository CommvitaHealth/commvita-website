We put everything commvita does on one page.

Three editions, and they stack. Flow is the operational base — 139 capabilities. Governance & Assurance adds 116. Population adds 162 and brings the clinical record with it. Every group is named, with how many things sit in it, so you can see the shape for yourself instead of taking my word for the size.

There’s a red box in the corner. It lists what we haven’t got: no ISO 27001, no Cyber Essentials, no toolkit return, no published penetration test. And we’re aligned to openEHR without being native to it — we write to an openEHR repository, ship no archetypes and have no AQL.

That box is there because it’s the first thing anyone technical goes looking for. Easier to print it than to have you find it.

Tell me what’s missing.
